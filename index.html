Ask us anything ↗
What does a managed IT partner actually do? +

We take responsibility for the health, security, and day-to-day support of your technology. That means proactive monitoring and maintenance, responsive help desk support, and a plan for the bigger picture—not just fixing things when they break.

Do you work with businesses our size? +

Cloudflex is built for growing teams that need enterprise-grade thinking without an enterprise-sized IT department. We’ll recommend the right level of coverage for your people and environment.

Can you work with our existing tools? +

Absolutely. We start with what you have, make it safer and more reliable, and only recommend change where it creates a clear advantage.

What happens in the free assessment? +

We’ll ask about your business, listen to what’s frustrating, and review the shape of your current setup. You’ll leave with useful observations and clear next steps, whether or not we work together.
